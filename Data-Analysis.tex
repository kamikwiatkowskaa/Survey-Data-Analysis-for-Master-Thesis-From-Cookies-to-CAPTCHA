\#Survey This is a test analysis of a dataset on

\#Hypotheses

\#\#Hypothesis 1 H1 Greater awareness of data extraction is not
associated with greater privacy-protective behaviour.

\#\#Hypothesis 2 H2 Greater awareness of data extraction is associated
with lower platform trust.

\#\#Hypothesis 3 H3 Greater awareness of data extraction is associated
with stronger support for regulation.

\#\#Hypothesis 4 H4 Users recognise their everyday online activity as a
source of economic value for platforms.

\#\#Hypothesis 5 H5 Stronger value recognition is associated with lower
perceived control, lower platform trust, and stronger support for
regulation.
